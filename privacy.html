FlowSynq

Privacy policy

Updated 2 October 2026

Everything stays on your iPhone

This version of FlowSynq has no server. Your account and everything you add are stored only on this iPhone, and nothing is sent to Abdalla Ahmed or anyone else.

What the app stores

Your name and the profile details you enter; your events, to-dos, reminders, checklists, plans and groups; and your settings. They’re protected by iOS file protection and included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backups, if you use them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and doesn’t share data with anyone.

Notifications, Face ID and photos

Notifications are scheduled on the iPhone itself. Face ID is checked by iOS; the app only learns whether it succeeded. If you choose a photo, only that photo is saved, on this iPhone.

TestFlight

Apple runs TestFlight. As a tester, Apple shares with Abdalla Ahmed your invite email, crash reports, your device model and iOS version, basic usage such as sessions and installs, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. Apple’s own privacy policy covers that.

Deleting your data

Settings › Account › Start from scratch erases what you’ve added. Delete account removes the account and its data from this iPhone. Deleting the app removes everything.

Changes

If a later version stores data online, for example to sync between devices, this policy will be updated and you’ll be told before anything leaves your iPhone.
